# Axolotl anatomy references (version 05)

The side-view proportions in `axolotl.mjs` (from version 05) come from comparing about 80 scientific plates, anatomy figures and specimen photos on Wikimedia Commons. The main ones:

- José María Velasco, *Study of Axolotl*, La Naturaleza, 1879 (lateral and anatomical figures): https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Study_of_Axolotl_by_Velasco_1879.jpg
- Duméril, Bibron & Duméril, *Erpétologie générale* (Ambystoma mexicanum plate): https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Erp%C3%A9tologie_g%C3%A9n%C3%A9rale,_ou,_Histoire_naturelle_compl%C3%A8te_des_reptiles_(Ambystoma_mexicanum).jpg
- Cuvier, *Humboldt Zoologie* plate XII (dorsal, ventral and gill anatomy): https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Anatomy-Ambystoma-mexicanum-Humboldt-Zoologie-T12p252.png
- AGTM D115, *The Axolotl – Siredon pisciforme*: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:AGTM_D115_The_Axolotl_-_Siredon_pisciforme.png
- Iconographia Zoologica, University of Amsterdam, Siredon prints (UBA01 IZ11400145–151)
- Side-view photos: "Ke - Ambystoma mexicanum" series, "Leucistic female Axolotl head side view" 1 and 2 (2010-02-24), "Axolotl Portrait", "Axolotl's head and gill", "Axolotl's foot" (all on Commons)

## Measurements used (total length 820 px, facing right)

- Head length about 0.21 of total length; tail (vent to tip) about 0.42.
- Eye about a third of the way back along the head, at mid-height; small and lidless.
- Mouth gape runs nearly straight back from the snout tip and turns up slightly just behind and below the eye. In profile only half the smile shows.
- Three gill rami per side, rising up and back from behind the head, with filaments along both edges.
- Forelimbs under the gills with four fingers; hindlimbs at the vent with five toes. Walking pose uses diagonal pairs.
- Dorsal fin starts as a low ridge behind the head and deepens over the tail; ventral fin runs from the vent to the tip.
