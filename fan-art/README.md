# Fan art: Lulu and Clawd

Fan animations of Lulu with Clawd, the Claude Code mascot, made for fun and for sharing on social media.

- **Clawd belongs to Anthropic.** Everything in `clawd/` is unofficial fan art of Anthropic's character, redrawn in Lulu's pixel style. It is **not** covered by this repository's CC0 dedication, and this project is not affiliated with or endorsed by Anthropic. Using Clawd is subject to Anthropic's rights in its character.
- **Lulu stays CC0.** The Lulu frames in this folder (`lulu-*.txt`) are Lulu's own art and CC0 like the rest of the repository.
- **The animations** (`scene.html` and anything exported from it) combine both, so they carry Clawd's restrictions too: share them as fan art and credit Clawd to Anthropic.

Clawd is left out of Lulu's sprite exports (`sprites/`). His own local sprites are built with `node tools/sprites.mjs fan-art/clawd/animations.json fan-art/clawd/sprites`.
