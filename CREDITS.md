# Credits

## Pixel Lulu

The pixel-art Lulu in this repository (sprites, turnaround views, experiments and the progress history) is dedicated to the public domain under [CC0 1.0](LICENSE). Use her, change her and share her however you like; no credit is required.

Lulu Axol'Otel was created for the [OTel Desktop Viewer](https://github.com/CtrlSpice/otel-desktop-viewer) project. The character, idea and pose are the project's own; the pixel art was drawn for the project.

## Original painting

The original Lulu painting (`reference.png`, also visible in some screenshots in `history/shots/`) was painted by [Chelsey Bateson](https://cbatesonart.artstation.com/) as a commission for the project. It is credited to her with thanks, and she keeps the right to show it in her portfolio.

The painting is not covered by CC0. It is part of [OTel Desktop Viewer](https://github.com/CtrlSpice/otel-desktop-viewer) and licensed with that project under the [Apache License 2.0](https://github.com/CtrlSpice/otel-desktop-viewer/blob/main/LICENSE).
